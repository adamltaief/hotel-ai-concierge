ABOUT SAHARA BEACH

Your Mediterranean Sanctuary of Sunshine & Smiles

Welcome to Sahara Beach AquaPark Resort, a hidden gem nestled along Monastir’s golden coastline. Set within 22 hectares of lush palm gardens directly on the Mediterranean Sea, our 3-star resort seamlessly blends authentic Tunisian charm with warm, genuine hospitality.

Ideally located just minutes from Monastir (8 km) and Sousse (12 km), and only 4 km from Monastir Airport, we offer the perfect balance of relaxation and adventure. Whether you’re splashing in our thrilling aquapark, savoring international cuisine, or relaxing on the beach, Sahara Beach is designed for creating unforgettable memories with family and friends.
