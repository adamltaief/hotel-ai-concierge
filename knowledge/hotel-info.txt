2. GENERAL DESCRIPTION
======================

Sahara Beach AquaPark Resort is a seaside resort in the Skanes area of Monastir, Tunisia.

The hotel is designed for holidays focused on relaxation, beach access, family holidays, entertainment, sports and all-inclusive stays.

The resort offers:

* Direct access to the seaside
* Sandy beach
* Large landscaped gardens
* Swimming pools
* Water games and waterslides
* Sports activities
* Day and evening entertainment
* Restaurants and snack bars
* Bars
* Spa and wellness services
* Free Wi-Fi
* Parking
* Children's activities

The hotel is suitable for couples, families and guests looking for a resort-style holiday.
